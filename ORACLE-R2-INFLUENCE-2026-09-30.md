## Hypothesis

Before the official draw, these six sets are fixed as the complete intervention set. The experimental hypothesis is that the eventual six main numbers of the Austrian Lotto 6 aus 45 draw on 2026-09-30 will exactly equal one of the six sets above.

Because all six candidate sets are distinct, an exact success can occur for at most one of them.

## Primary endpoint

SUCCESS = the official six main numbers for the 2026-09-30 Austrian Lotto 6 aus 45 draw exactly match one of the six fixed candidate sets above, with order ignored.

FAILURE = the official six main numbers do not exactly match any of the six fixed candidate sets.

## Scientific interpretation

This preregistration does not assume that intention, observation, retrocausality, or any other process can influence a lottery draw. It defines a falsifiable test. A success would be unusual and worth replication, but would not by itself establish a causal mechanism.
