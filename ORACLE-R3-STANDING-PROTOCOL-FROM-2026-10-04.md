# PROJECT ORACLE R3 — Standing Influence Protocol

**Effective from:** 2026-10-04

## Fixed candidate sets

1. 3 17 32 36 38 39
2. 3 5 17 32 39 43
3. 3 16 17 26 32 43
4. 18 29 34 38 42 45
5. 4 11 18 27 34 42
6. 2 7 22 27 38 42

## Scope

These six candidate sets are fixed in advance for every future Austrian Lotto 6 aus 45 drawing beginning with the regular Sunday drawing on 2026-10-04. The protocol applies to all regular Wednesday and Sunday drawings and also to any official Friday bonus drawing that occurs after this effective date.

## Primary endpoint per drawing

SUCCESS = the official six main numbers of that drawing exactly match one of the six fixed candidate sets above, with order ignored.

FAILURE = the official six main numbers do not exactly match any of the six fixed candidate sets.

No candidate set may be changed, added, removed, or reweighted after publication of this standing protocol. Any future change requires a new separately timestamped protocol that applies only to drawings after that new protocol is published.

## Interpretation

This is a preregistered repeated test. It does not assume that intention, observation, retrocausality, or any other process can influence lottery outcomes. Each drawing is evaluated against the same fixed success criterion, and all outcomes should be retained, including failures.
